Intellexa / Predator Spyware

Intellexa is the company behind the Predator spyware, a commercial surveillance tool used by governments to compromise mobile devices. The company faces growing legal pressure, including a lawsuit filed by Greek citizens in July 2026.

Legal Actions

  • Greek lawsuit (July 2026): Greek citizens filed a lawsuit against Intellexa alleging illegal surveillance using Predator spyware (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-09)
  • The case adds to growing legal pressure on commercial spyware vendors
  • Similar actions have been filed in Europe targeting other spyware vendors

Predator Spyware

Predator is a commercial spyware platform that: - Compromises mobile devices (iOS and Android) - Enables surveillance of communications, location, and device data - Has been linked to multiple governments' surveillance operations - Part of the broader commercial spyware ecosystem (alongside Pegasus from Nso Group)

Broader Context

  • Spyware was discovered on the phone of a European Parliament member who was actively investigating spyware abuses (July 3, 2026) (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-07)
  • The spyware ecosystem continues to face legal and regulatory challenges across Europe
