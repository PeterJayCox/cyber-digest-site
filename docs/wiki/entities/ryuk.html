Ryuk

Ryuk is a prolific ransomware variant and the associated cybercriminal operation that caused hundreds of millions of dollars in damages globally — including significant healthcare sector disruptions. In July 2026, a Ryuk operator pleaded guilty in the UK.

Law Enforcement Action

  • July 11, 2026: A Ryuk ransomware operator pleaded guilty in the UK
  • Demonstrates ongoing international cooperation in dismantling ransomware operations
  • Ryuk caused significant disruptions including healthcare sector incidents (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-11)

Historical Impact

  • Hundreds of millions in damages globally
  • Significant healthcare sector disruptions (Ryuk was heavily used against hospitals)
  • Typically deployed via TrickBot or BazarLoader infections
