IRIS C2

IRIS C2 is a startup that claimed to offer millions of dollars for zero-day exploits — but was revealed by Krebs On Security to be run by convicted felons and far-right conspiracy theorists.

Key Figures

Person Background
Jack Burkman Convicted felon; history of creating fake intelligence companies
Jacob Wohl Convicted felon; co-conspirator with Burkman on multiple fraudulent schemes

The pair have a long history of creating fake intelligence companies and spreading false claims. (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-09)

Corporate Structure

  • IRIS C2 is registered through Calvexa Group LLC
  • Calvexa is a federal contractor with no apparent active government contracts
  • The IRIS C2 operation appears to be the latest in Burkman and Wohl's pattern of creating fake security/intelligence companies

Significance

The Krebs investigation raises questions about: 1. Due diligence in the zero-day acquisition market 2. The ease with which convicted felons can create seemingly legitimate security startups 3. Whether any IRIS C2 customers existed or whether this was purely a fraud operation (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-10)
