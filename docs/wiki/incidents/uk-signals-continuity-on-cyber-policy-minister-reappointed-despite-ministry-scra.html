UK Cyber Policy Continuity — Minister Reappointed Despite Ministry Scrapping

Mayor Andy Burnham signalled continuity on UK cyber policy, reappointing the cyber minister despite the government's decision to scrap the dedicated ministry.

Summary

Despite the dissolution of a dedicated cybersecurity ministry, the UK government demonstrated policy continuity by reappointing the same cyber minister. This signals that cyber security remains a priority even as the government restructures its departmental responsibilities.
