New NatJack Attacks Hijack TCP Sessions and Spoof DNS by Manipulating NAT Tables

Researchers disclosed 'NatJack', a novel technique that manipulates NAT tables to hijack TCP sessions and spoof DNS responses, enabling network-level interception without compromising endpoints.

Key Details

  • Source: The Hacker News
  • Date: 2026-08-07
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Nature: Novel network-infrastructure attack technique

Summary

The attack takes advantage of stateful NAT behaviour to inject or divert traffic, posing risks to unencrypted DNS and TLS-upgrade flows.

Analysis

The disclosure adds to a growing body of network-infrastructure attack techniques relevant to enterprise and home-router security. It underscores the importance of encrypted DNS and proper NAT/firewall configuration hygiene.
