Suspected Chinese-Speaking Hackers Target Central Asian Governments With OctLurk and SilkLurk

Summary

Kaspersky identified a suspected Chinese-speaking threat group targeting government organisations across Central Asia since January 2025. Uses two new obfuscated backdoors (OctLurk, SilkLurk) delivered via LurkProxy.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-07-31
  • Source: The Hacker News
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Threat Actor: Suspected Chinese-speaking group (unnamed)
  • Target Countries: Afghanistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Syria
  • Target Sectors: Healthcare, research, foreign ministries, logistics, law enforcement, education
  • Tools: OctLurk, SilkLurk (obfuscated backdoors), LurkProxy (delivery)
  • Active Since: January 2025
