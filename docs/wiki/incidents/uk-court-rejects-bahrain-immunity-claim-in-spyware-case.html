UK Court Rejects Bahrain Immunity Claim in Spyware Case

Summary

A UK court has rejected Bahrain's claim of state immunity in a case involving the alleged use of spyware, marking a significant legal development in holding governments accountable for cyber surveillance tools deployed against dissidents and journalists.

Key Details

Field Detail
Jurisdiction United Kingdom
Country involved Bahrain
Subject Alleged spyware use
Ruling State immunity claim rejected
Date 2026-07-27

Significance

This ruling sets a precedent that foreign states may not be able to claim immunity when accused of deploying surveillance technoLogy against individuals — a legal avenue that could be applied to other spyware cases involving NSO Group's Pegasus, Intellexa's Predator, and similar tools. It represents a growing judicial willingness to pierce the sovereign immunity shield in the context of transnational cyber surveillance.
