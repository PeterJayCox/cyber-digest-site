MEPs Question Anthropic's EU Standing, Discuss Digital Sovereignty

European Parliament members raised questions about Anthropic's standing to operate in the EU, with discussions touching on digital sovereignty, AI regulation compliance, and the EU AI Act's implications for frontier AI developers.

Key Details

  • Source: IAPP
  • Date: 2026-07-26
  • Reliability: Tier 2 — Established cyber journalism
  • Entity: Anthropic (US-based AI company)
  • Questioners: Members of the European Parliament (MEPs)
  • Issues raised: Operational standing in the EU, digital sovereignty, EU AI Act compliance

Analysis

The questioning of Anthropic's EU standing reflects growing European concern about digital sovereignty and the dominance of non-European AI companies. As the EU AI Act takes shape, frontier AI developers — particularly those headquartered outside the EU — face increasing scrutiny over their compliance posture and the terms under which they operate in the European market.
