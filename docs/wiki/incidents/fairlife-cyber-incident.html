Fairlife Cyber Incident

Dairy company Fairlife suspended production at its US facilities following a cyber incident. The disruption affected supply chains, with the company working to restore operations.

Impact

  • Production suspended at US facilities
  • Supply chain disruption — dairy processing and distribution
  • Response and remediation ongoing

The incident highlights the vulnerability of food manufacturing to cyber attacks — a sector that has historically underinvested in OT security.

Australian Significance

Australia's dairy and food manufacturing sector faces similar OT security challenges. The Fairlife incident underscores the need for: - OT/ICS network segmentation in food processing facilities - Incident response plans for production-disrupting cyber incidents - Supply chain contingency planning for essential food infrastructure
