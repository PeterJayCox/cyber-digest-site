ACSC Publishes Guidance on Secure Adoption of Agentic AI in Defence

Summary

The Australian Signals Directorate's ACSC has published new guidance titled "Secure adoption of Agentic AI in defence", aimed at maximising cyber defence outcomes through responsible and secure agentic AI adoption. The publication addresses the emerging class of risks from AI agents operating autonomously — directly relevant to the week's cascade of frontier model breach disclosures from OpenAI and Anthropic.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-07-31
  • Source: ACSC
  • Reliability: Tier 1/4 — Official / first-party
  • Organisation: Australian Signals Directorate — Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC)
  • Document Title: Secure adoption of Agentic AI in defence
  • Relevance: Directly addresses risks materialised in OpenAI and Anthropic model-breach incidents
