Data Breaches Announced by Four Hospitals and Surgery Centres

Summary

Data breaches were reported by four US healthcare providers, continuing the steady stream of healthcare provider disclosures.

Affected Organisations

Organisation Type
Wildwood Surgical Center Surgery centre
Michigan Surgical Center Surgery centre
Penobscot Valley Hospital Hospital
Whitfield Regional Hospital Hospital

Context

This cluster of disclosures reflects the ongoing vulnerability of small and mid-sized healthcare providers to cyber incidents. Surgical centres and regional hospitals often operate with limited cybersecurity resources, making them attractive targets for data theft and ransomware.
