Senate Democrats Introduce Bill Distributing $300 Million Annually for Water System Cybersecurity

Senators Adam Schiff and Amy Klobuchar introduced the Water Cyber Shield Act, allocating $300 million per year of State Revolving Funds to water and wastewater cybersecurity.

Summary

Field Detail
Bill Water Cyber Shield Act
Sponsors Sen. Adam Schiff, Sen. Amy Klobuchar
Funding $300 million per year of State Revolving Funds for water and wastewater cybersecurity
EPA role Authority to regulate water-sector cyber protections
Requirements Risk assessments and corrective actions; incident-reporting obligations aligned with forthcoming CIRCIA rules
Context Cyberattacks on at least 30 water systems in ~12 US states, attributed by experts to Iran-linked groups
Date Introduced 2026-08-11

Key Provisions

  • $300 million per year of State Revolving Funds directed to water and wastewater cybersecurity.
  • Gives the EPA authority to regulate water-sector cyber protections.
  • Requires risk assessments and corrective actions.
  • Imposes incident-reporting obligations aligned with the forthcoming CIRCIA rules.

Significance

The bill follows cyberattacks on at least 30 water systems in about 12 US states attributed by experts to Iran-linked groups, capping a week that has consistently tied OT/ICS resilience to the highest-impact defensive stakes for water and wastewater systems.
