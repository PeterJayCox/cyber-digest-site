UK Account-Hack Losses Surge 417% as New Reporting System Exposes Hidden Cases

The City of London Police's first annual assessment under Britain's new Report Fraud platform found victims reported losing £6.3 million (US$8.5 million) to hacked email, social-media and other online accounts in the year to 31 March — up 417% from £1.2 million a year earlier — with the number of victims reporting a financial loss rising 929% (226 → 2,325).

Attribute Detail
Losses £6.3M (US$8.5M), up 417% YoY
Victims (financial loss) 2,325, up 929%
Driver Jan launch of Report Fraud (replaced Action Fraud) surfaced hidden cases
Source The Record — Tier 2/4

Police caution much of the jump reflects the January launch of Report Fraud surfacing previously-hidden cases, rather than a pure surge in attacks — a useful caveat when interpreting UK account-takeover loss statistics (relevant to the AU Scamwatch/cyber fraud analogy).
