MCBS Announces Cybersecurity Incident Impacting 1.26 Million Individuals

Summary

MCBS, LLC, an Augusta, Georgia-based healthcare management and revenue cycle management company, disclosed a major data incident affecting approximately 1.26 million individuals. The breach underscores persistent third-party vendor risk in the healthcare supply chain.

Key Details

Field Detail
Organisation MCBS, LLC
Location Augusta, Georgia, USA
Industry Healthcare management / revenue cycle management
Individuals affected ~1,260,000
Date disclosed 2026-07-27

Significance

As a third-party healthcare management provider, MCBS handles sensitive patient data on behalf of multiple healthcare organisations. This incident highlights the concentration risk inherent in healthcare supply chains — a single vendor breach can ripple across numerous downstream providers and patients.
