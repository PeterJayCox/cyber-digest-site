SilverFox Targets Japanese Manufacturer with 3-Driver BYOVD Chain and ValleyRAT

Summary

The Chinese cybercrime group Silver Fox (SilverFox) has been observed using new bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver (BYOVD) techniques targeting a Japanese industrial manufacturing organisation. The attack chain begins with an invoice-themed phishing lure, uses DLL sideloading via QQ and Tencent Cloud services, deploys three drivers for kernel access, and ultimately delivers ValleyRAT (Winos 4.0) for persistent remote access.

  • Exploitation: 3-driver bring-your-own-vulnerable-driver (BYOVD) chain for kernel access
  • Payload: ValleyRAT (Winos 4.0) — persistent remote access
