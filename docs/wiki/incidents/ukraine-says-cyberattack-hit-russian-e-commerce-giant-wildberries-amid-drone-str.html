Ukraine Says Cyberattack Hit Russian E-Commerce Giant Wildberries Amid Drone Strikes

  • Source: The Record
  • Date: 2026-08-16
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Entity: Wildberries (Russia's largest online marketplace)

Summary

Ukraine's military intelligence (HUR) claimed it disrupted the operations of Russia's largest online marketplace, Wildberries, in a cyberattack intended to amplify the impact of drone strikes on the company's infrastructure. The operation was reportedly carried out with the Cyber Corps hacker group and caused "widespread disruption" affecting customer service, contact centres and payment infrastructure.

Key Facts

  • HUR claimed the operation targeted Wildberries' digital infrastructure (customer service, contact centres, payments)
  • Run in cooperation with the Cyber Corps hacker group
  • Intended to amplify drone-strike impact on company warehouses
  • HUR said it targeted the company due to its role in Russia's logistics network and financing the war
  • Claims could not be independently verified
