CISA/FBI Joint Advisory — Russian State-Sponsored Zimbra Phishing Campaign (Aa26 204A)

A joint cybersecurity advisory from CISA, FBI, and international partners detailing a Russian state-sponsored phishing campaign targeting users of the Zimbra Collaboration Suite.

Summary
