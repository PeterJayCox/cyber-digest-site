Federal Agencies Broaden Alert on Iran-Linked OT Attacks

US federal agencies issued a broadened alert on Iran-linked attacks targeting operational technoLogy (OT) environments, expanding previous guidance on ICS/SCADA threats.

Summary

Field Detail
Threat Actor Iran-linked APT groups
Target Operational TechnoLogy (OT) / ICS / SCADA environments
