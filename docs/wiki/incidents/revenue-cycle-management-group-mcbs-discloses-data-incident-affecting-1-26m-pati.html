Revenue Cycle Management Group MCBS Discloses Data Incident Affecting 1.26M Patients

Summary

Healthcare billing and revenue cycle management vendor MCBS disclosed a massive data breach exposing sensitive records of 1.26 million patients.

Details

Augusta, Georgia-based healthcare and billing vendor MCBS, LLC announced a major cybersecurity incident that compromised the sensitive personal and medical data of approximately 1.26 million patients.

Breach Characteristics

The breach involved unauthorised access to corporate file servers. The exposed data includes: - Patient names - Clinical identifiers - Insurance billing records - Demographic information

Response Actions

MCBS is currently co-operating with law enforcement and preparing notification letters for all affected patients. This event highlights the persistent risks third-party vendors introduce to healthcare infrastructure and sensitive patient details.
