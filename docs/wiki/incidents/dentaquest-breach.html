DentaQuest Healthcare Breach — 15+ Million Notified

Dental benefits administrator DentaQuest began issuing notification letters to over 15 million individuals affected by a cybersecurity incident discovered in May 2026. This is one of the largest healthcare data breach notifications in recent years.

Overview

Attribute Detail
Target DentaQuest — US dental benefits administrator
Sector Healthcare — dental insurance/benefits
Impact 15+ million individuals notified
Discovered May 2026
Notification July 23, 2026
Status Investigation ongoing; full scope still being assessed

Significance

  • Among the largest healthcare data breaches on record by number of affected individuals
  • Dental benefits data typically includes personally identifiable information (PII), insurance details, and protected health information (PHI)
  • The scale underscores the concentration risk when a single dental benefits administrator processes data for millions
