CISA Urges Water and Wastewater Systems Sector to Protect OT Against Activity Targeting PLCs

Summary

CISA issued an urgent alert urging the Water and Wastewater Systems Sector to protect OT environments against activity targeting PLCs, following the coordinated cyberattack disabling OT at 30+ Minnesota water systems.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-07-30
  • Issuing body: CISA (US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency)
  • Target sector: Water and Wastewater Systems Sector
  • Threat: Activity targeting programmable logic controllers (PLCs)
  • Trigger: Coordinated cyberattack disabling OT at 30+ Minnesota water systems
  • Source: CISA
  • Reliability: Tier 1/4 — Official / first-party

Significance

The alert is directly relevant to Australian water utilities regulated under the SOCI Act and the ACSC's CI Fortify guidance on OT isolation, and to NZ water infrastructure under the NCSC's critical infrastructure framework. The Minnesota water system attacks demonstrate the real-world risk of OT-targeted cyberattacks against critical water infrastructure.
