AA26-204A — CISA/FBI Joint Advisory on Russian Zimbra Phishing Campaign

AA26-204A is a joint cybersecurity advisory published July 25, 2026 by CISA and international partners detailing a Russian state-sponsored phishing campaign targeting users of the Zimbra Collaboration Suite.

Summary

The advisory provides:

  • Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
  • Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs)
  • Mitigation guidance for organisations using Zimbra

Campaign

Russian state-sponsored threat actors are conducting targeted phishing operations against Zimbra webmail users. The advisory is a coordinated international effort to raise awareness and provide defensive guidance.
