Patients Warned About AnMed Communications After Cyberattack Closes 83 Facilities

Summary

The Anderson, South Carolina-based nonprofit health system AnMed confirmed it is continuing to make progress restoring its systems after a cyberattack forced the closure of 83 facilities. The operational disruption demonstrates the real-world impact of healthcare cyberattacks on patient access to care.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-08-03
  • Target: AnMed (nonprofit health system, South Carolina)
  • Impact: 83 facilities closed due to cyberattack
  • Status: Systems restoration in progress
  • Source: HIPAA Journal
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism

Significance

This incident is part of the most concentrated single-sector attack volume since the Cyber Digest series began — four separate healthcare incidents across the US in a single day (Amgen, CareCloud, AnMed, Health-ISAC ShinyHunters alert). The operational disruption to patient care highlights the acute physical-world consequences of healthcare cyberattacks.
