Stade Français Cyberattack — French Rugby Club Systems Restored

Stade Français, a top-tier French rugby union club, confirmed a cyberattack that forced system restoration. The club is investigating a potential data leak. The incident adds to the growing trend of sporting organisations being targeted by cybercriminals, following recent attacks on other European sports entities.
