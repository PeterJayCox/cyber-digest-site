PNLD Breach Exposes UK Police and Government Contact Details on Dark Web

Summary

The Police National Legal Database (PNLD) confirmed that police, government and customer contact information was compromised and published on the dark web. The data included names, organisations and work email addresses belonging to police officers, police staff, criminal justice professionals, and government partners.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-08-03
  • Target: Police National Legal Database (PNLD), UK
  • Data exposed: Names, organisations, work email addresses
  • Affected groups: Police officers, police staff, criminal justice professionals, government partners
  • Publication: Dark web
  • Source: The Hacker News
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism

Important Distinction

PNLD is not the Police National Computer or the Police National Database, and does not hold confidential intelligence or investigation data. However, the exposure of named officer contact details could make phishing messages targeting those individuals appear more convincing.

Significance

The breach enables highly targeted phishing campaigns against UK law enforcement and government personnel, potentially facilitating further compromise of police and government systems through social engineering.
