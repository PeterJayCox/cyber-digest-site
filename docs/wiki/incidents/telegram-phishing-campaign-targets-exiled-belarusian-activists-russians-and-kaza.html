Telegram Phishing Campaign Targets Exiled Belarusian Activists, Russians, and Kazakhs

Summary

A coordinated Telegram phishing campaign has been observed targeting exiled Belarusian political activists alongside Russian and Kazakhstani nationals. The operation's infrastructure and targeting patterns suggest state-linked intent.

Key Details

Field Detail
Platform Telegram
Primary targets Exiled Belarusian political activists
Secondary targets Russian nationals, Kazakhstani nationals
Attribution State-linked (suspected)
Date reported 2026-07-27

Significance

The targeting of exiled Belarusian activists points to transnational repression via cyber means — a pattern consistent with state-sponsored campaigns aimed at dissidents operating from abroad. The inclusion of Russian and Kazakhstani nationals broadens the campaign's scope beyond a single diaspora.
