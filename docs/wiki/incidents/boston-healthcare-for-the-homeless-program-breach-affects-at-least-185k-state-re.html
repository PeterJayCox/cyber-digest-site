Boston Health Care for the Homeless Program Breach Affects at Least 185K State Residents

  • Source: HIPAA Journal
  • Date: 2026-08-14
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established sector journalism

Summary

A data breach at the Boston Health Care for the Homeless Program affects at least 185,000 Massachusetts residents. The extent and nature of the data exposed are still being assessed; the incident is reported alongside several other US healthcare disclosures.

Key Facts

  • At least 185,000 Massachusetts residents affected
  • Occurred at a program serving homeless populations
