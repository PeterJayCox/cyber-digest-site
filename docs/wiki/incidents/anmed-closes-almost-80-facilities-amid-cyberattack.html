AnMed Closes Almost 80 Facilities Amid Cyberattack

Summary

AnMed, a nonprofit health system serving upstate South Carolina and Northeast Georgia, was forced to close nearly 80 facilities while responding to a cyberattack affecting its networks. The incident disrupted patient care services across the region.

Key Details

Field Detail
Organisation AnMed (nonprofit health system)
Location Upstate South Carolina / Northeast Georgia, USA
Facilities affected ~80
Date disclosed 2026-07-27
Service impact Patient care services disrupted

Impact

The closure of approximately 80 facilities represents a significant regional healthcare disruption. The full scope of data compromise, if any, has not been detailed in initial reporting.
