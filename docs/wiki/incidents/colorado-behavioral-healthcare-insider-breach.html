Colorado Behavioral Healthcare Provider Discovers Insider Data Breach

Multiple healthcare providers announced breaches including NAS Recovery Solutions, Entyre Care Massachusetts, Carle Health, and Brown Health Medical Group-MA. Insider threat remains a persistent vector in the sector.

Significance

Insider breaches in healthcare are particularly damaging because of the sensitivity of medical and mental health records. Multiple providers being affected simultaneously suggests either a coordinated attack vector or a systemic vulnerability in healthcare data access controls.
