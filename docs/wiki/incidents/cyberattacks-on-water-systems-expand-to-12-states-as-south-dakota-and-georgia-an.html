Cyberattacks on Water Systems Expand to 12 States

Cyberattacks targeting water systems have expanded to 12 US states, with South Dakota and Georgia the latest to announce incidents. CISA separately urged the water and wastewater sector to protect OT environments against activity targeting programmable logic controllers (PLCs).

Attribute Detail
Scope Water-system cyberattacks in 12 US states
Latest South Dakota, Georgia
CISA guidance Protect water/wastewater OT against PLC-targeting activity
Assessment Escalation from single-state incidents to a coordinated, multi-state campaign
Source The Record / CISA — Tier 2/4

The spread marks an escalation from single-state incidents to a coordinated, multi-state campaign against critical water infrastructure — directly relevant to AU/NZ critical-infrastructure resilience (SOCI Act / Australia's water sector).
