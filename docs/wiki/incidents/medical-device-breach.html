Medical Device Manufacturer Breach

A major medical device manufacturer sent breach notifications to nearly 4 million individuals, making this one of the largest healthcare sector data breaches of the year. The identity of the manufacturer was not disclosed in reporting.

Details

  • Victim: Major medical device manufacturer (undisclosed)
  • Individuals notified: ~4 million
  • Sector: Healthcare / Medical device manufacturing
  • Date: July 6, 2026
  • Source: The Record

Significance

  • One of the largest healthcare data breaches of 2026
  • Medical device manufacturers hold sensitive patient data alongside device data
  • Healthcare sector continues to be a prime target for cyber attacks (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-07)

Australian Angle

Medical device manufacturers operating in Australia should review their security posture. Australian healthcare organisations using devices from affected manufacturers may be indirectly impacted by credential exposure or device configuration compromise. The Oaic Notifiable Data Breaches scheme may apply if Australian patient data is involved. Au Impact
