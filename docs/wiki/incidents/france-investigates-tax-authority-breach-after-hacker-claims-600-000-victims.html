France Investigates Tax Authority Breach After Hacker Claims 600,000 Victims

  • Source: The Record
  • Date: 2026-08-14
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Entity: France DGFiP (tax authority)

Summary

The French General Directorate of Public Finances (DGFiP) data breach has been confirmed by the Economy Ministry. The attacker used a stolen or misused identity to gain access in late June, viewing and extracting data on individuals and businesses. A hacker using the moniker 'ZeroBytes' claimed 600,000 victims, although this figure is unverified. DGFiP is to notify affected individuals, notify the CNIL (data protection regulator), and file a complaint.

Key Facts

  • Breach confirmed by France's Economy Ministry
  • Attacker used a stolen/misused identity; access began late June
  • Data on individuals and businesses viewed/extracted
  • Hacker 'ZeroBytes' claims 600,000 victims (unverified)
  • DGFiP to notify affected, notify CNIL, and file a complaint
