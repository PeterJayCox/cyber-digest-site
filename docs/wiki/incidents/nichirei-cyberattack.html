Nichirei Cyberattack — Food logistics Disruption

Japanese food logistics giant Nichirei was hit by a cyberattack that disrupted cold-chain logistics operations. An extortion group claimed responsibility. The company is recovering operations.

Overview

Attribute Detail
Target Nichirei (Japanese food logistics company)
Sector Manufacturing — food logistics/cold-chain
Impact Cold-chain logistics disruption
Perpetrator Extortion group (claimed responsibility)
Status Recovering operations
Date 2026-07-22

Significance

This incident highlights ongoing OT/IoT risks in the food supply chain sector: - Cold-chain logistics are critical infrastructure for food safety and supply - Disruption to temperature-controlled logistics can result in spoilage and supply chain cascades - Manufacturing/OT systems are increasingly targeted by extortion groups
