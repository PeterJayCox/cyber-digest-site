Brown Health Medical Group-MA Data Breach Affects 312,000 Individuals

Summary

Lifespan Physicians Group of Massachusetts, doing business as Brown Health Medical Group-MA, confirmed that the protected health information of approximately 312,000 individuals was compromised in a data breach. The Massachusetts-based healthcare provider has begun notifying affected patients.

Details

The incident adds to a concentrated period of healthcare breach activity following the Amgen, CareCloud, and AnMed disclosures. The compromised data includes protected health information (PHI) which falls under Hipaa regulatory requirements. Affected individuals have been notified, and regulatory filings are expected to follow. The breach underscores the persistent targeting of the healthcare sector by cybercriminals seeking access to sensitive medical records and personally identifiable information.
