Beverly Hills Plastic Surgeon Confirms Data Theft/Extortion Incident

  • Source: HIPAA Journal
  • Date: 2026-08-17
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Entity: Terry J. Dubrow, MD (Beverly Hills, CA plastic surgery practice)

Summary

Terry J. Dubrow, MD, a Beverly Hills, California-based plastic surgeon, notified the California Attorney General about a security incident in which an individual claimed to have breached its computer systems and copied sensitive patient information. An investigation confirmed unauthorised access to parts of its network starting 16 January 2026, with files copied; on 27 July 2026 the practice confirmed patients' personal information had been obtained.

Key Facts

  • Individual claimed to have breached the practice and copied patient data
  • Unauthorised access confirmed from 16 January 2026; files copied
  • Data theft confirmed 27 July 2026 — names, patient-chart data, referring-physician info
  • Potentially contact details, Social Security numbers, driver's licence/state IDs, birth dates, prescription information
  • Practice notified the California Attorney General
