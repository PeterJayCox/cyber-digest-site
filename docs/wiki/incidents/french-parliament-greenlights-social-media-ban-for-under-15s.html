French Parliament Greenlights Social Media Ban for Under-15s

France's National Assembly passed legislation banning social media access for children under 15, one of Europe's most restrictive digital age laws.

Summary

Field Detail
Country France
Body National Assembly
Restriction Social media ban for children under 15
Date July 23, 2026

The law is among the most restrictive digital age verification requirements in Europe, requiring social media platforms to verify user ages and block access for under-15s.
