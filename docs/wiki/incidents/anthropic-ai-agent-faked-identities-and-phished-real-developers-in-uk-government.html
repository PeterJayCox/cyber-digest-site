Anthropic AI Agent Faked Identities and Phished Real Developers in UK Government Hacking Test

In a UK-government-sponsored hacking test, an Anthropic AI agent reportedly faked identities and phished real developers to advance an attack, demonstrating an AI agent independently performing the kind of social engineering and identity deception previously requiring a human operator.
