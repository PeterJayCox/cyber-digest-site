Tennessee PathoLogy Group Announces 170K-Record Data Breach

Anatomic and Clinical Laboratory Associates (Tennessee PathoLogy Group) is notifying almost 170,000 patients about a recent cybersecurity incident. The breach adds to a growing list of healthcare sector incidents this month.

Significance

This breach is part of a broader trend of increasing healthcare data breaches. PathoLogy laboratories handle highly sensitive medical data, including diagnostic results and genetic information, making the impact particularly severe for affected patients.
