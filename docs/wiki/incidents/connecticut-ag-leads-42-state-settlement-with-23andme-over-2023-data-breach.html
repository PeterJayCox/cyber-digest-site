Connecticut AG Leads 42-State Settlement With 23andMe Over 2023 Breach

A coalition of 42 state attorneys general, led by Connecticut Attorney General William Tong, reached a settlement with the bankruptcy trustee for 23andMe, resolving claims from the company's 2023 data breach that exposed genetic and personal data of millions of customers.

Summary

Field Detail
Parties 42 state attorneys general (led by Connecticut)
Defendant 23andMe (via bankruptcy trustee)
Reason 2023 data breach exposing genetic and personal data
Date July 23, 2026

This settlement follows the earlier 23Andme Settlement reached with individual states.
