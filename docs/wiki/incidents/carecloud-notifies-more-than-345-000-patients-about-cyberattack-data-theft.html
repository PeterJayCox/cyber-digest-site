CareCloud Notifies More Than 345,000 Patients About Cyberattack Data Theft

Summary

CareCloud Inc., a New Jersey-based provider of cloud-based and AI-powered EHR, RCM, PM, and clinical documentation solutions, began notifying over 345,000 patients that their data was compromised in a cyberattack.

Details

The incident underscores the concentration risk in healthcare IT vendors — a single compromise cascades across multiple provider organisations. CareCloud's platform serves a large number of healthcare providers, making this a significant supply-chain breach in the healthcare sector.
