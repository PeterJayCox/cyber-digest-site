UK Information Commission Takes Shape with Non-Executive Board Appointments

The UK's reconstituted Information Commission announced non-executive board appointments while DSIT (Department for Science, Innovation and Technology) opened consultations on data use reforms, signalling continued evolution of the UK's post-Brexit data protection framework.

Key Details

  • Source: IAPP
  • Date: 2026-07-26
  • Reliability: Tier 2 — Established cyber journalism
  • Entity: UK Information Commission (reconstituted)
  • Related body: DSIT — data use reform consultations

Context

The appointments and consultations mark another step in the UK's development of an independent data protection regime following its departure from the EU. The trajectory of these reforms will have implications for UK adequacy decisions and international data transfer arrangements.
