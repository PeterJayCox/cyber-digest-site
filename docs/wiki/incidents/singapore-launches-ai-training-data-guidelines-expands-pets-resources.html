Singapore Launches AI Training Data Guidelines, Expands PETs Resources

Singapore released new AI training data guidelines alongside expanded privacy-enhancing technoLogies (PETs) resources, positioning itself as a leader in AI governance frameworks in the Asia-Pacific region.
