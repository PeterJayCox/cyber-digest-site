Global Data Breach Cost Rises 12% to Almost $5 Million — IBM 2026 Study

Summary

The IBM 2026 Cost of a Data Breach Study reports that the average cost of a data breach has risen 12% year-on-year to nearly $5 million globally. The study also highlights a surge in mega breaches (1M+ records), malicious insider incidents, and the growing financial impact of healthcare data breaches.
