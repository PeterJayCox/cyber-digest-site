China's Regulation on AI Companions Takes Force

China's new regulation governing AI companion applications took effect, imposing requirements on developers of conversational AI systems regarding content moderation, data privacy, and user protection.

Key Details

  • Source: IAPP
  • Date: 2026-07-26
  • Reliability: Tier 2 — Established cyber journalism
  • Jurisdiction: China (People's Republic of China)
  • Scope: AI companion / conversational AI applications
  • Requirements: Content moderation, data privacy, user protection

Context

China continues to build out its comprehensive AI regulatory framework. This regulation specifically targets AI companion apps — a growing category of conversational AI products — and imposes obligations around what these systems can say, how they handle user data, and how users must be safeguarded.
