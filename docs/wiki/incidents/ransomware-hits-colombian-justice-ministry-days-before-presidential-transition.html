Ransomware Hits Colombian Justice Ministry Days Before Presidential Transition

Colombia's Ministry of Justice was hit by a ransomware attack days before a presidential transition, disrupting systems at a politically sensitive moment. No major data-loss claims have been substantiated, and recovery was ongoing at disclosure.

Key Facts

Attribute Detail
Victim Ministry of Justice, Colombia

Impact Systems disrupted; data-loss claims unsubstantiated
Confidence Incident confirmed via official/press reporting; data-loss claims unverified
Disclosure date 2026-08-12

Context

The timing — during a government handover — is the analytically significant element: transition periods create gaps in security ownership, monitoring continuity and response capability, making them high-risk windows for disruptive attacks. It extends a fortnight of public-sector ransomware activity (US local governments, the Gunra advisory) and is a warning for AU/NZ organisations facing leadership, vendor or platform transitions: explicitly hand over security ownership and revalidate monitoring before changeovers.
