SafePal Crypto Hardware Wallet Maker Confirms Breach Affecting Nearly 40,000 Customers

  • Source: SafePal (vendor disclosure)
  • Date: 2026-08-16
  • Reliability: Tier 1/4 — Official / First-party (vendor disclosure)
  • Entity: SafePal (crypto hardware wallet manufacturer)

Summary

Crypto hardware wallet company SafePal confirmed a data breach, telling users that nearly 40,000 customers had information stolen during a recent security incident. The incident impacted people who placed orders between 2 March 2025 and 11 April 2026, affecting names, email addresses, shipping addresses, phone numbers and purchase details.

Key Facts

  • ~40,000 customers affected (order data via a plug-in flaw)
  • Flaw in the order-tracking function allowed unauthorised access to another customer's order information
  • Incident remediated; wallets, seed phrases and private keys confirmed secure
  • SafePal is the third hardware-wallet maker attacked in the last month (after Trezor and Coinkite)
