'Wrench' Attacks Against Crypto Holders Appear to Be on the Rise

Physical coercion attacks ("wrench attacks") targeting cryptocurrency holders are increasing, with criminals using violence or the threat of violence to force victims to transfer digital assets.

Overview

Attribute Detail
Date 2026-07-24
Type Physical coercion / violent crime
Target Cryptocurrency holders
Source The Record
Reliability Tier 2/4 — High

Significance

The rise of wrench attacks represents a shift from purely cyber-enabled crime to physical violence targeting crypto wealth. Key concerns include: - Victims are identified through on-chain analysis, social media, or physical surveillance - Irreversible cryptocurrency transactions make recovery nearly impossible - Increasingly sophisticated targeting of known crypto investors - Blurs the line between cybercrime and traditional violent crime

Prevention Recommendations

  • Avoid publicly disclosing crypto holdings or wallet addresses
  • Use cold storage with multi-signature schemes
  • Implement time-locked transactions and withdrawal limits
  • Consider custody solutions for significant holdings
