ACSC Publishes New AI Frontier Cyber Threat Guidance for Boards of Directors

Summary

The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC), jointly with the Australian Institute of Company Directors (AICD), published new guidance on frontier AI cyber threats tailored for boards of directors. The guidance provides structured questions that directors can ask to understand and oversee AI-related cyber risks within their organisations.

Details

This guidance complements the earlier "Secure Adoption of Agentic AI in Defence" guidance and reflects the accelerating pace of AI security governance publications from Five Eyes nations following recent high-profile AI incident disclosures. The guidance is directly relevant to all Australian enterprises governed by Apra Cps 234, the Privacy Act, or the ASX Corporate Governance Council's principles. Boards are encouraged to use this guidance as a benchmark for board-level AI risk oversight. The publication represents the first major AU domestic cybersecurity publication in over a week.
