US Senator Demands Immediate Elimination of Legacy VPNs Across Federal Networks

Summary

Following a series of costly corporate and federal gateway compromises, a US Senator has publicly demanded that federal networks purge traditional virtual private networks.

Details

A US Senator has publicly called for federal agencies to completely purge outdated virtual private networks (VPNs) from their environments.

Security Concerns with Legacy VPNs

The Senator highlighted that legacy perimeter remote-access gateways represent fragile, single-point-of-failure entry vectors. These entry points are actively and aggressively targeted by nation-state actors and advanced persistent threats.

Recommended Direction

The Senator urged a rapid architectural transition toward Zero Trust remote-access architecture and modern remote-access programmes to mitigate the vulnerability of the federal enterprise footprint.
