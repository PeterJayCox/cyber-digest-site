AA26-231A — Active-Threat Advisory on US Siemens S7 PLCs

The NSA, FBI, CISA, Department of Energy and EPA issued an active-threat advisory (AA26-231A) warning that unidentified actors are conducting reconnaissance and capability development against US Siemens S7 Series programmable logic controllers (PLCs) — used across energy, water and agriculture — using AI-generated exploitation scripts disguised as legitimate monitoring tools, alongside exploitation of known vulnerabilities.

This is a rare joint active-threat advisory against industrial controllers, formalising AI-generated-script use against OT and urging operators to isolate PLCs from the internet.
