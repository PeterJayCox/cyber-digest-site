Finland to Disconnect Fibre-Optic Link to Russia as Lease Expires

Summary

Finland will disconnect its last remaining direct fibre-optic telecoms link to Russia as the lease expires — a hard cut in digital connectivity between the NATO member state and Russia, removing a potential vector for Russian signals intelligence.

Key Details

  • Date: 2026-07-31
  • Source: The Record
  • Reliability: Tier 2/4 — Established cyber journalism
  • Action: Disconnection of last remaining direct fibre-optic telecoms link
  • Parties: Finland (NATO member) / Russia
  • Reason: Lease expiration
  • Significance: Removes a potential vector for Russian signals intelligence (SIGINT)
