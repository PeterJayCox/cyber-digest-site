BONK Cryptocurrency Governance Attack

Attackers compromised a governance mechanism in the Solana-based Bonk cryptocurrency and voted themselves approximately $20 million worth of tokens.

Attack Details

  • Platform: Solana blockchain
  • Token: BONK
  • Method: Governance mechanism compromise
  • Value extracted: ~$20 million in BONK tokens
  • Date: July 6, 2026

Significance

This incident highlights ongoing security challenges with crypto governance protocols — the mechanisms by which token holders vote on project decisions. When governance is compromised, attackers can vote themselves treasury funds or mint new tokens. (raw/digests/Cyber-Digest-2026-07-07)
