CargoNet Reports $31.8M in Labour Day Freight Theft

Fraud-intelligence firm Verisk CargoNet warned ahead of the US Labour Day holiday that cargo theft in the annual seven-day windows has climbed 70% over five years (273 incidents 2021–2025, estimated commodity value ~$31.8 million), and highlighted a growing identity-based scheme.

Attribute Detail
Scheme Criminals breach trusted freight channels by compromising carrier accounts, email, business phones or compliance platforms, then alter delivery instructions after a legitimate carrier has taken possession
Regional concentration California, Texas, Illinois (~half of incidents)
H1-2026 losses Exceed $359 million
Source FreightWaves — Tier 3/4

The item is a fraud-and-identity data point driven by account/credential compromise rather than malware. Note: this is largely a physical/logistics fraud story with a cyber (credential-compromise) component — the identity-theft element is the relevant cyber dimension.
