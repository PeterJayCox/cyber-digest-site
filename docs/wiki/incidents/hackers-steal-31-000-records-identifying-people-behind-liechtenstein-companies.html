Hackers Steal 31,000 Records Identifying People Behind Liechtenstein Companies

Summary

Threat actors compromised systems holding registry data for Liechtenstein-based companies and foundations, stealing approximately 31,000 records containing identifying information of beneficial owners and company officers.

Details

The breach exposes the privacy vulnerabilities of jurisdictions marketed for financial discretion. The stolen records contain identifying information of beneficial owners and company officers behind Liechtenstein corporate structures.
