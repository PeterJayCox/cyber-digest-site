Illinois AI Safety Measures Act (SB 315)

The Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act (SB 315) was signed by Illinois Governor JB Pritzker, making Illinois the third US state to enact comprehensive safety and transparency requirements for developers of the most advanced AI systems.

Key Provisions

Attribute Detail
Bill SB 315 — Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act
Jurisdiction Illinois, USA
Signed 2026-07-17
Scope Developers of frontier AI models
Requirements Safety testing, transparency reporting, risk mitigation

Significance

Illinois joins a growing list of US states (following California and Colorado) that have enacted standalone AI safety legislation in the absence of comprehensive federal AI regulation. Key patterns:

  1. Frontier AI focus — Targets the most capable models, similar to California's approach
  2. Safety testing mandates — Required pre-deployment testing and evaluation
  3. Transparency reporting — Mandated disclosures about model capabilities and limitations
  4. Risk mitigation — Developers must implement safeguards against foreseeable harms

Australian Relevance

While US state-level regulations do not directly apply in Australia, the trend toward AI-specific legislation is relevant for: - Companies operating in both US and Australian markets - The Australian government's own AI regulation framework development - Setting emerging international norms for frontier AI governance
