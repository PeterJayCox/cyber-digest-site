China's Regulation on AI Companions Takes Force

New Chinese regulations governing AI companion applications have taken effect, establishing requirements for content moderation, data protection, and age verification for AI-driven social and companion apps.

Overview

Attribute Detail
Date 2026-07-24
Jurisdiction China
Scope AI companion applications
Requirements Content moderation, data protection, age verification
Source IAPP News
Reliability Tier 2/4 — High

Key Requirements

  • Content moderation: AI companion apps must implement content filtering to prevent generation of harmful, illegal, or otherwise prohibited content
  • Data protection: Stringent requirements around collection, storage, and processing of user data by AI companion services
  • Age verification: Mandatory age verification systems to protect minors from inappropriate AI interactions

Significance

China's regulation of AI companions is among the first dedicated regulatory frameworks for this rapidly growing category of AI application. As AI companions become more sophisticated and widely adopted globally, this regulation may serve as a reference model — or cautionary example — for other jurisdictions considering similar rules.
