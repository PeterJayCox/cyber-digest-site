Singapore Launches AI Training Data Guidelines, Expands PETs Resources

Singapore released new guidelines on AI training data governance and expanded resources for privacy-enhancing technoLogies (PETs), positioning itself as a leader in AI governance in the Asia-Pacific region.

Overview

Attribute Detail
Date 2026-07-24
Jurisdiction Singapore
Action AI training data guidelines + PETs resource expansion
Source IAPP News
Reliability Tier 2/4 — High

Key Elements

  • Training data governance guidelines: Standards for collection, curation, and use of AI training data, including consent, bias mitigation, and transparency requirements
  • PETs resource expansion: Enhanced tools and guidance for privacy-enhancing technoLogies including differential privacy, federated learning, and synthetic data generation

Significance

Singapore continues to position itself as a regional leader in AI governance, offering a regulatory model that balances innovation with privacy protection. This follows similar initiatives from other Asia-Pacific jurisdictions and contrasts with the legislative gridlock observed in the US.
