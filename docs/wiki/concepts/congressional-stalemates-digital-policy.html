Congressional Stalemates Take Wind Out of US Digital Policy Sails

Ongoing congressional gridlock is stalling US digital policy initiatives, including comprehensive federal privacy legislation. The IAPP analysis notes that state-level activity continues to fill the vacuum.

Overview

Reliability Tier 2/4 — High

Significance

The ongoing congressional stalemate means the US continues to lack comprehensive federal privacy and AI regulation. This has several consequences: - State-level patchwork: States like California, Illinois, and others continue to pass their own privacy and AI laws (e.g., Illinois Ai Safety Act) - Regulatory uncertainty: Businesses face inconsistent requirements across states - International divergence: The US falls further behind the EU (Nis2 Directive) and Asia-Pacific jurisdictions in comprehensive digital regulation - Enforcement gaps: Federal agencies lack clear statutory authority for emerging technoLogy oversight
