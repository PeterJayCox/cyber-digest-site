The flaw allowed local AI sessions to break out of their Linux virtual machine sandbox to read or write files anywhere on the host Mac, exposing sensitive user credentials and SSH keys without prompting for user permission before it was patched. - Source: The Hacker News - Reliability: Tier 2 — High - Date: 2026-07-23

3. Chaos Ransomware Uses Rust-Based msaRAT to Funnel C2 Traffic via Headless Chrome - Summary: Cisco Talos researchers detailed a highly sophisticated command-and-control (C2) evasion technique deployed by the Chaos ransomware syndicate via a Rust-based implant called msaRAT. Instead of making outbound C2 connections, the malware launches headless instances of Chrome or Edge and drives them via the Chrome DevTools Protocol to route all communications over WebRTC channels via Twilio TURN service, making malicious traffic indistinguishable from normal browsing on the network. - Source: The Hacker News - Reliability: Tier 2 — High - Date: 2026-07-23
